Rafu privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026

In short: Rafu keeps your shop's records on your phone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. Rafu has no account, no server and no advertising.

What Rafu stores

The records you enter: products and their photos, stock movements, sales, customers and what they owe, payments you log, end-of-day cash counts, and your shop's name, logo and contact details. They are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.

What Rafu does not do

  • It does not send your data to us or to anyone else.
  • It has no analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It does not ask to read your SMS or notifications. A payment message is read only when you share it to Rafu or paste it, and only the extracted details (amount, provider, masked phone number, transaction number, time) are kept, never the message itself.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used only when you choose to take a product photo or a supplier receipt photo. Text on receipts is read on your phone. Barcode scanning uses the scanner provided by Google Play services on your phone.

The microphone is used only while you sell by voice, after you tap the microphone button and allow it. Speech is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service; Rafu receives only the text. Depending on your phone's settings, that service may be provided by Google.

Sharing and backups

Receipts, payment reminders and supplier orders are shared only when you tap share, and only to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp.

A backup is one file that you create and save where you choose: your phone, a memory card, a cloud drive you use, or a message to yourself. Rafu does not upload it anywhere. Rafu's records are excluded from automatic cloud backup.

Children

Rafu is a business tool for shop owners and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
